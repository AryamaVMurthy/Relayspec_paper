Speculative drafters that use a target model's hidden states are trained for a particular target. A service may activate a LoRA adapter or switch to a model of a different size or family, changing the hidden states supplied to the drafter. RelaySpec reuses the pretrained drafter by learning one linear map for each target feature stream while keeping the drafter transformer frozen. The maps fold into its existing fusion projection before inference. For LoRA-fine-tuned targets, we train the maps on target-generated responses. For cross-transfer, we train them to reconstruct the original target's features. We evaluate mathematical reasoning, circuit design, coding, and chat workloads. On some LoRA-fine-tuned targets, RelaySpec improves DFlash throughput by up to 85\% over the unadapted drafter. In Qwen3-4B to Qwen3-8B cross-transfer, it reaches up to 100.2\% of native-drafter throughput on MATH and 99.4\% on GSM8K. The same approach generalizes to EAGLE-3 drafters, where it improves throughput by 46\% to 106\% over the unadapted version on LoRA-fine-tuned targets.
