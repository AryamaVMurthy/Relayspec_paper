RelaySpec trains linear maps on the hidden states supplied to a frozen drafter and folds them into its fusion projection for inference. The maps improve throughput on all tested LoRA-fine-tuned targets. In Qwen3-4B to Qwen3-8B cross-transfer, they approach native-drafter throughput on Math and GSM8K, with weaker results on Code, Chat, and cross-family transfers. The same input-map approach also improves a frozen EAGLE-3 drafter. Matched controls show that separate maps train better than one equally sized fused projection in the tested settings, while the analysis identifies useful changes in context direction. The drafter transformer stays shared across targets, though cross-transfer at very small data budgets and high-concurrency serving remain limited.
